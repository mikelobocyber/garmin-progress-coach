Upload Garmin files

CSV exports, .fit files, or the .zip from "Export Original" — pick several at once. Runs, lifting, cycling, cardio, walking, hiking, mobility, and more.

Where do I find these files?

CSV (many activities): Garmin Connect → Activities → All Activities → Export CSV (top right).

FIT (one activity, richer data): open an activity in Garmin Connect → gear icon → Export Original. The downloaded .zip can be uploaded as-is.

From your watch: plug it in via USB and copy files from GARMIN/Activity/. Non-activity files are skipped automatically.
